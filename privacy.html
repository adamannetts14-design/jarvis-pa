PRIVACY

Jarvis is a private personal assistant used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.

With the owner's permission it reads the owner's own Google Calendar and Gmail, adds calendar events the owner asks for, and writes email drafts that the owner sends themselves. It never sends email on its own.

What it reads stays on the owner's computer. It isn't sold, shared or used for advertising, and nobody else's data is collected.

The owner can disconnect Google at any time from Jarvis's settings, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
